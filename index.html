I am a cultural sociologist who studies the intersection of art, culture, and politics. Currently, I am a fourth-year PhD student at the University of Virginia, Department of Sociology. In my dissertation research, I study the Kurdish contemporary artistic practice, specifically focusing on socially and politically engaged contemporary art. I hold an MA in Sociology from the University of Virginia, and a BA in Sociology from Middle East Technical University.
