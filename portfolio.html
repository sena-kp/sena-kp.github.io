This page lists the DH projects I have worked on and am currently working on. I see these projects as complementary and inspiring to my academic work although they are not direct results of my dissertation research and not always central to my academic writing practice.

Kurdish Art and Politics: A Digital Archive of Contemporary Kurdish Art

(2025-ongoing)

Kurdish Art and Politics is a digital archive project that I am currently working on. The project aims to document and archive contemporary Kurdish art and its intersection with politics. The complete archive will include artworks, artist statements, exhibition catalogs, and other relevant materials.
